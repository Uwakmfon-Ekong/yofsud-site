About us

Built by young people, for the communities they call home.

The Youth Forum for Sustainable Development (YOFSUD) is a youth-led organization established to empower young people and communities through sustainable development initiatives.

We work with schools, rural communities, government institutions, development partners, civil society organizations, and the private sector to implement projects that promote environmental sustainability, climate resilience, quality education, public health, biodiversity conservation, and youth empowerment.

Our work aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals and contributes to building resilient, inclusive communities across Nigeria and Africa.

Climate action Conservation Education Public health

Our vision

A future where every young person is empowered to lead sustainable development — building resilient, inclusive, and environmentally sustainable communities.

Our mission

To educate, inspire, and empower young people through innovation, environmental conservation, research, advocacy, leadership development, and community-based initiatives that improve lives and protect the planet.

Focus areas

Six fronts, one goal.

Climate change

Promoting climate education, adaptation, mitigation, and resilience through community engagement and youth participation.

Environmental conservation

Protecting forests, wetlands, biodiversity, and ecosystems through restoration, tree planting, and advocacy.

Youth empowerment

Developing young leaders through entrepreneurship, mentorship, leadership training, and green skills.

Education

Promoting Education for Sustainable Development, environmental literacy, and SDG awareness in schools.

Public health

Supporting health promotion, disease prevention, sanitation, hygiene, and health information in underserved areas.

Circular economy

Encouraging waste reduction, recycling, responsible consumption, and plastic pollution prevention.

Ongoing projects

Where the work is happening right now.

Classroom SDG session
PROJECT 01

Niger Delta SDG Educate Project

Promotes Sustainable Development Goals education in primary, secondary, and tertiary institutions across the Niger Delta — sustainability clubs, environmental debates, teacher training, and leadership programmes.

Education · SDG awareness
Coastal plastic clean-up
PROJECT 02

Niger Delta Ocean Save Project

Addresses marine plastic pollution through plastic recovery, recycling, environmental awareness, and marine animal rescue with coastal communities, volunteers, and schools.

Marine conservation
Rural health outreach
PROJECT 03

Community Health Information Network (CHIN)

Improves access to health and safety information in rural, marginalized, and coastal communities — disease prevention, hygiene, WASH, maternal and mental health.

Public health
Mangrove restoration
PROJECT 04 · FLAGSHIP

Youth Initiative on Environment and Local Development (YIELD)

Planting One Million Economic Trees in schools and communities and delivering the Niger Delta Mangrove Restoration Project — ecosystem restoration, carbon sequestration, and sustainable livelihoods.

Ecosystem restoration
